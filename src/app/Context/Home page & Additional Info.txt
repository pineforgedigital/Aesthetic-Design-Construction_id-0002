ROOTED IN FAITH. BUILT FOR THE FUTURE.
What began as individual gifts has become a shared calling.
Aesthetic Design & Construction was born from a belief that the gifts we are given are meant to be shared.

We believe God has uniquely gifted each of us with different talents, passions, and abilities—and brought us together in a way that allows us to use those gifts to create something meaningful.

My husband’s passion for craftsmanship was passed down through his father, who now brings his own artistic talent and love of woodworking to our team as our expert custom craftsman.

My love of decorating, design, and bringing an entire vision to life became the creative foundation of the business.

And a friendship that began with helping Kelley design her basement years ago grew into a creative partnership built on a shared ability to envision, develop, and create beautiful spaces together.

Today, our gifts come together through family and friendship, creating something we hope will last far beyond any individual project.

We are building a business our children can grow up watching us create. A family-owned company rooted in the community we grew up in. Something built on faith, hard work, creativity, craftsmanship, and pride—something our family can one day look back on and be proud to call our own.

We feel incredibly blessed to have the opportunity to use the gifts God has given us to serve others through work we genuinely love.
