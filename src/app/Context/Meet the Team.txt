MEET THE PEOPLE BEHIND AESTHETIC
Aesthetic Design & Construction was built around a simple belief: we each have different gifts, and there is something special about bringing those gifts together.

Our team combines formal design education, hands-on remodeling experience, project management, construction knowledge, artistic creativity, and generations of craftsmanship—all with a shared commitment to creating beautiful spaces and an exceptional experience for our clients.

KEELIN CANNON
Founder | Creative Director | Interior Designer
Keelin’s passion for design began early, with four years of fashion design and a year of interior design during high school. She went on to earn her associate degree in Interior Design while working as an interior designer at a furniture store, where she discovered her love for helping clients turn their ideas into beautiful, livable spaces.

After completing her degree, Keelin joined a remodeling company where she gained hands-on experience in nearly every side of the business. She was trained in remodeling, estimating, project management, scheduling, and design—giving her a unique understanding of both the creative and construction sides of a project.

Today, Keelin oversees the design direction and management of Aesthetic Design & Construction, guiding projects from the initial concept through completion. She works closely with clients to understand how they live, what they envision, and how their home can best serve them. She also oversees project coordination, ensuring the many details behind each project come together seamlessly.

Keelin has a natural eye for proportion, color, materials, and the details that make a space feel finished. She loves seeing the big picture while also understanding how every individual decision contributes to the final result.

Her gift: Seeing the potential in a space and bringing every detail together to create a cohesive vision.

KELLEY
Interior Designer | Decorating Partner
Kelley brings an artistic eye, a natural talent for decorating, and a lifetime of creativity to the Aesthetic team.

Her career has been rooted in finance and budgeting, where she developed a strong understanding of organization, planning, and managing details. She is also a retired member of the Navy, bringing with her the discipline, dependability, and attention to detail that have become such a valuable part of who she is.

But decorating has always been a natural gift.

Kelley first met Keelin when she came into the furniture store where Keelin was working, looking for help designing her basement and selecting furniture. What began as a design project quickly became a friendship, and over the years, the two discovered just how naturally their creative minds work together.

Kelley has an incredible ability to see a space’s potential and bring warmth, personality, and character into a room. She and Keelin have a unique creative partnership—often seeing the same vision from different perspectives and building upon one another’s ideas to create something even better.

Today, Kelley is Keelin’s decorating partner, helping clients bring their spaces to life through furnishings, artwork, textiles, accessories, and the finishing details that make a home feel complete.

Her gift: Creating warmth and personality through the details that turn a beautiful space into a home.

CHRISTIAN CANNON
Founder | Project Planning & Construction
Christian brings a hands-on understanding of construction, a background in sales and marketing, and a passion for making sure the moving pieces behind every project come together seamlessly.

His knowledge of craftsmanship began at home, spending years alongside his father, Mark, in the woodshop and learning the fundamentals of building, creating, and working with his hands. He also learned the basics of electrical work and developed an appreciation for the skill and patience required to create something well.

Christian went on to study marketing and has built his professional career in sales, developing strong communication, relationship-building, and organizational skills that now play an important role in Aesthetic Design & Construction.

Behind the scenes, Christian helps with project planning, coordination, scheduling, and overseeing the many details that keep a project moving forward. You’ll also find him in the field whenever an extra set of hands is needed—from demolition and installation to moving materials, jobsite cleanup, and whatever else the project requires.

His goal is to make sure the work gets done, the details don’t get missed, and each project continues moving smoothly from one phase to the next.

His gift: Bringing people, plans, and details together to keep a project moving forward.

MARK
Master Craftsman | Custom Woodworking
Mark is the craftsman behind the custom woodwork at Aesthetic Design & Construction, bringing more than 30 years of self-taught woodworking experience and a lifelong appreciation for quality craftsmanship.

His professional background began in the electrical trades at just 18 years old, giving him a broad understanding of construction and making him something of a true jack-of-all-trades. While his electrical expertise remains an important part of his knowledge, his role with Aesthetic is centered primarily around what he loves most: creating beautiful things from wood.

Over the years, Mark has taught himself to create everything from bedroom and dining furniture to cabinetry, mantels, built-ins, and custom architectural details. If you can imagine it, there’s a good chance he can figure out how to build it.

Mark approaches every piece with the same philosophy: start with quality materials, build it to last, and don’t compromise on the finish.

He takes the time to find beautiful, durable wood and carefully considers how each piece should be constructed so it will remain strong and functional for years to come. His attention to detail and pursuit of a flawless finish are what make his work truly custom.

For Mark, woodworking isn’t simply about building something.

It’s about creating a piece that becomes part of a home—and has the potential to be enjoyed for generations.

His gift: Turning raw materials into beautifully crafted, enduring pieces made specifically for your home.
